I wanted somewhere to put the things I'd forget by lunchtime. A tablet to take. A call to make. The three small jobs that get the morning going.

In BlobLife you can type or dictate a sentence and turn it into a reminder, a habit or a routine. Today puts it alongside the rest of your day. You can leave yourself a note for tomorrow.

<img src="/projects/blob-life/today-november.png" alt="BlobLife Today screen with a note from last night and three morning tasks" />

I built it in Flutter. The earlier version was organised around four phases and a blob that reacted to them. The current screen is simpler. I wanted to see what was due and get on with it.
