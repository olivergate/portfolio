## Make it easy to get back to

If I miss a day, I want the app to help me pick up the next one. Making me feel bad about a broken streak would be a pretty good way to make sure I never opened it again.

## Put the next thing in view

I tried organising the day into four fixed phases. The current design starts with Today. I can see the next item, add another one and get back to what I was doing.
