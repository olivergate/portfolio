## Put it somewhere you'll see it

Type or say what you need to do. Check what the app made of it and save it. Today shows what's next, including the note you left the night before.

## Make the morning small enough to start

Give a routine a time and a few steps. Move the steps around if the order is wrong. On Today you can tick them off as you go.

## Reminders and notes

For something you can't miss, a reminder can ask again until you acknowledge it. There's also a journal for things you want to write down.
